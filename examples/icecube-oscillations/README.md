# IceCube: atmospheric $\nu_\mu$ disappearance

**Field:** neutrino physics · **Used in:** extra case for self-study

**Question.** What are the oscillation parameters ($\Delta m^2_{32}$, $\sin^2\theta_{23}$) and their uncertainties?

**Source.** IceCube three-year muon-neutrino disappearance data release (arXiv:1410.7227; https://icecube.wisc.edu/science/data/nu_osc).

**Real measurement.** IceCube Collaboration, atmospheric muon-neutrino disappearance with three years of DeepCore data: [arXiv:1410.7227](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.7227).

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `data/icecube.dat` | 64 bins (8 × 8), 5118 events. Columns: lower and upper edge of a zenith variable (−1 to 0), lower and upper edge of an energy variable (0.80–1.75), counts |

## Suggested path (self-study)

- **After Lab 2:** the model (survival probability, see the original exercise), baseline fit; MINOS errors and the correlation of the two parameters; contour.
- **After Lab 3:** nuisance parameters (normalisation, spectral index) as constraint terms; effect on the intervals.
- *Caveat to watch:* intervals near the physical boundary ($\sin^2$ close to 1).
- Check the exercise text for the units of the energy and zenith variables.
