# Top quark: on/off counting

**Field:** collider · **Used in:** Lab 2 (Exercise B), Lab 3 (Exercises A and B)

**Question.** 17 events are observed in the signal region. A control region measures the background. What is the significance, and what is the interval on the signal?

**Real measurement.** D0 Collaboration, “Observation of the Top Quark”, Phys. Rev. Lett. 74 (1995) 2632: 17 events observed, expected background 3.8 ± 0.6. [arXiv:hep-ex/9503003](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/9503003), [doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.74.2632](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.74.2632).
