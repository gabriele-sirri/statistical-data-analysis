# OPERA: $\nu_\tau$ appearance

**Field:** neutrino physics · **Used in:** Lab 3 (Exercise C)

**Question.** Four decay channels of the $\tau$, ten candidates. How significant is the excess, and what is the signal strength $\mu$ when systematic uncertainties are shared between channels?

**Source.** OPERA, final results on $\nu_\tau$ appearance, arXiv:1804.04912, Table III. The 2015 discovery paper (arXiv:1507.01417) lists the signal systematics: model 10 %, oscillation parameters 10 %, tagging efficiency 15 %.

**Real measurement.** OPERA Collaboration, final results on ντ appearance: [arXiv:1804.04912](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.04912); discovery paper: [arXiv:1507.01417](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.01417).

**Assumption of the exercise (ours, not the collaboration's).** Each source is fully correlated across the channels it affects; the covariance matrix is diagonalised into independent nuisance parameters.
The channel totals in the paper are consistent with independent channels. The counting model gives about 3.9σ; the paper's 6.1σ uses the kinematic information of each candidate.
